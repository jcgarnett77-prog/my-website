I spent £13,000 on solar. Was it actually worth it?

18 panels, a Tesla Powerwall 3 and a lot of questions. This is the ongoing story of what I bought, why I bought it and whether the numbers make sense.

Level 7Overthinking Scale

Figures reflect the project details currently known. Performance and savings will be added from actual readings.

It started with an electricity bill.

Jeffs Bay is a holiday let, so electricity use is not quite like a normal home. Guest changeovers, occupancy, heating and hot water all affect the picture. There were already four solar panels, but I wanted to understand whether a much larger system with battery storage could make a meaningful difference.

Why spend £13,000?

The new installation includes 18 × 490W panels, a Tesla Powerwall 3, Gateway, EV Wall Connector and an Eddi energy diverter. The decision wasn't just about generating electricity: it was about storing it, using more of it on site and understanding export options.

The questions that matter

How much electricity will the system really generate? How often will the battery fill? What will be imported and exported? How much will the tariff change the result? And what happens when the house is full of guests?

What I'm tracking

I'll use actual generation, import, export, battery behaviour, bills and tariff data. Forecasts will be labelled as forecasts; measured results will be labelled as measured results. No invented payback claims.

The verdict — not yet

Let's get some real-world data first.

This is an ongoing investigation. The honest answer will come after enough time has passed to see how the system performs through different seasons and occupancy levels.
